About the Business

Profitable, fully equipped beauty salon for sale in the vibrant Azizi Riviera community, offering a turnkey opportunity with established clientele.
Step into a fully operational beauty salon in the vibrant and fast-growing Azizi Riviera community. Spanning 460 sq. ft., this salon is designed to offer a seamless beauty experience, featuring a dedicated lashes room, 3 pedicure and 4 manicure stations, 1 hairdresser station, a pantry, and a private washroom. Every corner of the salon is optimized for efficiency and client comfort, making it easy to start operations immediately.

Located in a lively community with ample parking for clients, this salon attracts consistent foot traffic and has an established clientele. With an annual rent of AED 140,000 and a selling price of AED 270,000, it presents a rare turnkey investment opportunity for beauty professionals or investors seeking a ready-to-run business in a prime Dubai location.

This salon is ideal for those looking to step into the beauty industry without the hassle of setup and initial marketing, offering both convenience and profitability from day one.
